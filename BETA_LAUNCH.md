# Beta launch

## Seven-day build sequence

1. Day 1 — Scaffold, design system, homepage, pricing
2. Day 2 — Auth, onboarding, migrations, RLS
3. Day 3 — Mock providers, odds comparison, EV math + tests
4. Day 4 — Dashboard, bet tracker, bankroll
5. Day 5 — AI assistant, Stripe entitlements
6. Day 6 — Docs, hardening, deploy staging
7. Day 7 — Smoke tests, invite 25–50 beta users

## Fourteen-day acquisition

1. Soft-launch landing page with waitlist/signup
2. Share in sports-analytics communities (no spam)
3. Creator partnerships: research tooling disclosure, not pick-selling
4. Referral concept: credit toward Pro month for successful paid referrals (compliance review)
5. Pricing test: Pro $24.99 vs $29.99 for a subset
6. Track: visitor→signup, signup→trial, trial→paid, retention D1/D7, AI cost/user

## Copy guidelines

Honest research positioning. No guaranteed-profit claims, fake testimonials, or fabricated win rates.

## Support

Contact form → support inbox. Bug triage: severity (blocker / major / minor), owner, ETA. Feedback button in-app (follow-up).

## Roadmap from feedback

Prioritize data freshness, scanner UX, and bankroll alerts before arbitrage/player props/creator workspaces.
