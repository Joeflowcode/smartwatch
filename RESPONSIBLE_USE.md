# Responsible use

EdgePilot AI is an analytics and educational tool.

## Required notices

- Platform does not accept wagers or custody funds
- No guaranteed outcomes or “locks”
- Users must follow local law and meet legal gambling age
- Odds/stats may be delayed or inaccurate
- Historical performance ≠ future results
- Model outputs are informational estimates
- Never wager money you cannot afford to lose

## Product constraints

No loot boxes, spin wheels, fake urgency, fake social proof, confetti after wagers, streak rewards for betting, or loss-linked bonuses.

## Resources

Configurable by region on `/responsible-use` (NCPG, 1-800-GAMBLER, Gambling Therapy). Expand the map as jurisdictions launch.

## Bankroll tools

Conservative defaults (2% max stake, quarter Kelly). Loss-limit breaches show warnings, disable upsells on that page, and encourage a break.
